Safety

LPG Safety Comes First

Check for leaks

If you suspect a gas leak, avoid flames and electrical switches and move away from the area safely.

Keep cylinders upright

Gas cylinders should be kept upright in a suitable, stable location.

Keep away from heat

Keep LPG cylinders away from excessive heat and ignition sources.

Use proper equipment

Use appropriate regulators, hoses and appliances designed for LPG.

Get professional help

Do not attempt dangerous repairs or modifications yourself.
